08

Usage Guidelines

✅
Do
  • Use the green mark on light backgrounds
  • Maintain minimum exclusion zone (20% of mark width)
  • Ensure wordmark alignment to cap-height
❌
Don't
  • Do not distort or stretch the mark or wordmark
  • Do not apply outlines or dropshadows to the logo
  • Never use the mark and wordmark together
