08

Usage Guidelines

✓

Do

  • Use the green mark on white, off-white, pale yellow, khaki, lime, and teal sage surfaces
  • Use the white mark on forest green, deep green, and charcoal backgrounds
  • Maintain clear space equal to the cap-height of the "b" letterform on all sides
  • Use the circle badge for avatars, profile images, and app icons
  • Use the full lockup (mark + wordmark) as the primary brand placement
✕

Don't

  • Place the green mark on dark backgrounds — use the white version instead
  • Place the white mark on light backgrounds — it will be lost
  • Stretch, distort, rotate, or skew the logo or wordmark
  • Use colours outside the official palette as logo backgrounds
  • Apply drop shadows, outlines, glows, or other effects to the mark
