Practical Tips 🗒

💳 Currency
Hungary uses Hungarian Forint (HUF) — cards widely accepted, but carry cash for markets and playgrounds. ATMs are plentiful in the centre.
🚌 Getting Around
Budapest has excellent public transport. Buy a 24hr or 72hr travel pass for unlimited rides. Trams 2 and 19 along the Danube are scenic. Uber and Bolt both work well.
🌡 April Weather
Expect 8–17°C with a mix of sunny and cloudy days. Light jacket essential for mornings and evenings. Rain showers possible — pack a small umbrella.
📱 Useful Apps
BKK Futár — Budapest public transport planner. Bolt — local rideshare, often cheaper than Uber. Google Maps works excellently for navigation.
